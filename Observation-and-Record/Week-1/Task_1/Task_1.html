About Our Institution

ANITS was established in the academic year 2001-02 by Dr. N.B.R. Prasad in memory of his late son Anil Neerukonda. It is situated in a sprawling 12-acre green campus at Sangivalasa, Visakhapatnam.

The campus features advanced research centers focusing on reaction kinetics such as H2O synthesis and computing speeds up to 109 operations per second.

ANITS Admission Process (Ordered List)

  1. Qualify in the AP EAPCET / ECET entrance examination.
  2. Participate in online web counseling conducted by APSCHE.
  3. Select ANITS (College Code: ANIT) as your preferred choice.
  4. Report to the ANITS Sangivalasa campus for document verification and fee payment.
